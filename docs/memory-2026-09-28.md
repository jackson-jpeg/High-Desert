# Memory on the VPS, 2026-09-28

At 21:32 UTC Claude Code stopped a background job in the High Desert session
(the watch for the first nightly mutation run) because the box was low on
memory. This is what was using it. It was measured read-only; nothing was
stopped or changed apart from what is listed under "What changed".

## The box

Measured at 22:02 UTC.

| | |
|---|---|
| RAM | 7.8 GB total, **3.0 GB available (38%)**, 1.6 GB free, 2.0 GB cache |
| Swap | **12 GB already**: `/swapfile2` 10 GB (2.5 GB used, priority 10) and `/swapfile` 2 GB (unused, priority -2) |
| `vm.swappiness` | 60 (the default) |
| Committed | 5.7 GB of a 15.9 GB limit |
| CPUs | 2 |
| OOM kills | none today (atop `oomkill 0` in every sample) |

There was swap, so the "no swap: add a 4 GB swapfile" step did not apply, and
**nothing about swap was changed**. Swappiness stays 60. Lowering it was part of
the no-swap step. With 12 GB of swap and 9.7 GB of it free, the pressure at
21:32 was a burst in RAM, not a shortage of swap.

| 21:28 | 1.1 GB | 285 MB | the same, plus `tsc --noEmit` in `/root/sogojet-wt-dep-vercelog` (539 MB) |

| 21:31 | 2.5 GB | 1.2 GB | a second Actions `jest --coverage`; `eslint .` in `sogojet-redesign-merge` (433 MB) |

| 21:33 | 1.0 GB (13%) | 304 MB | the same jest runs |

It was not one process. In a seven-minute window, up to five test and build
jobs from three projects were running at once, each 0.4 to 1.8 GB, on top of
about 3 GB that is always resident. One of the five was mine: High Desert's
deploy build at 21:26, run at `nice -n -15`. Available memory went down to
13 to 14%, and Claude Code reaped an idle session's background shell.

## For you to decide (nothing here was stopped or changed)

The processes that are not High Desert's, with what they used:

1. **SoGoJet's Actions runner** (`actions-runner-sogojet`): 1.76 GB for one
   `jest --coverage`, twice in the 21:26 to 21:33 window. It is the largest
   single process on the box. Options:
   - `MemoryHigh=` on the unit, so the kernel reclaims from it first;
   - `--maxWorkers=1` / `--workerIdleMemoryLimit` for jest;
   - leave it.
2. **SoGoJet worktree test runs from Claude sessions**
   (`sogojet-redesign-merge`, `sogojet-wt-dep-vercelog`): jest 1.2 to 1.3
   GB each, tsc 0.5 GB and eslint 0.4 GB, overlapping with CI.
3. **Four resident Claude sessions** at 0.5 to 0.9 GB each. The two idle for
   days are `claude-leftsaid-1` (up 4 days) and `claude-screenreceipts-1`
   (up 3 days).
4. **Per-session MCP servers**: about 220 MB each. They could be shared.
5. **`docket4me-next` and `sanger-next`**: about 210 MB each already swapped
   out, meaning idle. `plotslop` holds 265 MB resident.

**My own part:** a High Desert deploy (`next build`, about 0.4 GB, at nice
-15) was one of the five concurrent jobs. From now on, the status `memory`
line gives a deploy a place to look first.

## What changed

- **`highdesert-status` has a `memory` line**: `MemAvailable` as a share of
  `MemTotal`, WARN under 15%, FAIL under 5%, with swap in use beside it,
  reported and not judged (`scripts/status.sh`, tests in `status.test.ts`,
  mutations `status-memory-*`). It is a point-in-time reading. At 21:32 it
  would have read WARN (14%), and at 22:02 it reads OK (38%).
- Swap: unchanged (see above).
- Nothing else was stopped, restarted or reconfigured.
