# The two looks: how to switch, and what is different

Both looks run on the real site, highdesert.space, with real data. Only you can
see them. Everyone else gets today's site, unchanged.

## How to switch (on your phone or any browser)

1. **Sign in once on that device.** Open the admin sign-in link in the folder
   `high-desert-KEY-2026-10-12` in your Downloads. It works once, within 24
   hours, and keeps you signed in for 30 days. (Need another? Ask for "a new
   High Desert admin link".)
2. **Open https://highdesert.space/look** and tap **Clean** or **Win98**. The
   page reloads in that look.
3. **Flip any time** with the small **Look:** button on every page (top right on
   a phone, bottom right on a desktop). **Public (today)** puts you back on the
   site everyone sees.

The look lasts 30 days on that browser. Signing in on the phone does not change
the laptop, and the other way round.

## Clean: today's site, done properly

Same night sky, desert amber, Win98-flavoured dark chrome. What changed:

1. **Calmer chrome.** Thin hairline borders instead of heavy bevels, rounded
   windows with a soft shadow, quiet title bars, one button style everywhere,
   an amber focus ring.
2. **Type with a hierarchy.** Fewer sizes, used consistently; bigger, clearer
   numbers on Stats.
3. **The phone tab bar** is taller, with real icons, and the mini player is a
   floating rounded card with big round buttons.
4. **The Library** reads like a clean table on desktop (hairline rows, an amber
   edge on the open show, green on the one playing) and like spaced cards on a
   phone. The search box, show tabs and category chips are proper controls.
5. **The detail panel** is flat and wider, with Play as the one amber action.
6. **Menus, the command palette, context menus and toasts** are rounded and
   consistent.
7. **Radio is gone as a tab.** The Library has a **List / Dial** switch: Dial
   spins the radio dial through the catalog (filters apply). Old /radio links
   land on the Dial.

## Win98: the real thing

The same layout in authentic gray Windows 98.

1. **Gray Windows 98 everywhere**: black text on button gray, real 3D bevels,
   square corners, navy-to-blue title bars, gray scrollbars, dotted focus boxes,
   a teal desktop.
2. **A taskbar and a Start menu** replace the tabs. The Start menu (with the
   "High Desert 98" band) has Library, Live, Phone Lines, Dial, Stats, About,
   Shortcuts, Help, Run... and Shut Down... The tray shows the clock and who is
   online. Shut Down ends on "It's now safe to turn off your radio."
3. **The player is Winamp**: green LED clock, scrolling title, visualizer,
   classic buttons, volume and seek sliders, and PL opens the queue as a
   playlist window. Full-screen Winamp on a phone.
4. **The Phone Lines are a Notepad window** ("Phone Lines - Notepad"): a white
   log where each call is a line like `[10:42 PM] Line 3, Skeptic from Reno: ...`.
   Report, rename and your admin tools are in the Edit menu and on right-click.
5. **The Library** is a white Explorer-style list with raised column headers;
   welcome, 404 and error pages are dialog boxes.
6. **Radio is gone as a tab** here too: Dial is in the Start menu and the
   Library.
7. **Mel's Hole** is hidden. Type `hole` on any page with nothing selected, or
   Start, Run..., then `melshole`. (Help has a hint.)

## The biggest differences, side by side

| | Public (today) | Clean | Win98 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feel | dark, busy chrome | dark, calm, consistent | gray, nostalgic, playful |
| Navigation | tabs (with Radio) | tabs (no Radio), icons on phone | taskbar and Start menu |
| Player | dark bar | flat bar, floating card on phone | Winamp |
| Phone Lines | chat panel | chat panel, tidied | Notepad log |
| Radio | its own tab | Dial mode in the Library | Dial mode in the Library |
| Show pages | dark | dark, refined | gray window |
